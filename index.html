Remlio BLE Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how Mohamed Darkaoui, publishing apps as Learnova Apps ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use Remlio BLE (the "App"). The App is a Bluetooth remote-control utility and does not require an account.

1. Information used by the App

  • Bluetooth data: With your permission, the App uses Bluetooth to discover compatible devices, show paired devices, connect to them, and send remote-control input. It may process a device name, Bluetooth address, and connection status on your device to provide these features. The App does not send this information to Learnova Apps servers.
  • Permissions: Bluetooth permissions are used for scanning and connecting. On Android 11 and earlier, Android may require location permission to allow Bluetooth scanning; Remlio BLE does not use that permission to determine your geographic location. Notification permission is optional on Android versions that support it.
  • Local settings: The App stores preferences such as language, appearance, and control mode, plus your favorite-device list and the address selected for automatic connection, in the App's local data area on your device.

2. Ads and Google services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display App Open, banner, and interstitial ads, and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices and consent where applicable. We do not operate our own advertising server.

Depending on ad settings, your region, and your choices, Google's SDK may collect and share information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), advertising and device or account identifiers, usage interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad views, and diagnostic or performance information. This data may be used for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Whether ads are personalized or non-personalized depends on your consent, privacy settings, and applicable regional requirements. The App updates consent through UMP before requesting ads. When privacy options are available, you can open them from the App's Settings. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android settings.

3. How information is used and shared

Bluetooth data and local settings are used to provide remote-control features and remember your preferences. We do not sell your data, and the current version does not provide user accounts or an App backend operated by us. Google Mobile Ads receives data collected by its SDK as described above; Google's terms and policies govern its processing. If you open an external link, such as the source-code repository, the visited website's privacy policy applies.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

Your preferences and saved device addresses remain in the App's local storage until you remove them in the App, clear the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Android manages paired devices; you can unpair them through the App or system settings. Google retains advertising data under its own policies and tools; we do not control Google's retention periods.

5. Security

App preferences are stored in Android's app-specific sandbox. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we do not control Google's infrastructure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny Bluetooth permissions in Android settings; device discovery and connection features may then stop working.
  • You can delete local App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can change ad and privacy choices through the UMP form when available, and manage your advertising ID in Android.
  • For questions, requests, or concerns about this policy or the App's privacy practices, contact the developer at learnovaapps@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose remote-control utility and is not specifically designed for children. We do not create accounts or directly collect personal information from children. Ads and treatment of under-age users depend on the publisher's and Google's applicable settings and policies; audience settings in Play Console and AdMob should match the App's actual audience.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date. App: Remlio BLE. Developer: Mohamed Darkaoui. Publisher name: Learnova Apps. Contact email: learnovaapps@gmail.com.
